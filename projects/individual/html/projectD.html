Model Car Refinish

Two different models will be painted. Firstly, an orange Porsche GT3 RS will be repainted. In addition to changing the color to blue, the spoiler will be removed and some details will be changed or added with J-B Weld WaterWeld. The metal body has been sanded and primed, and an airbrush with enamel or laquer will be utilized for even metallic coating. The other model, a 911 Carrera 3.2 targa is a plastic body which does not need to be sanded but does need to be glued to be put together. It will mainly be painted metallic red.

Planning Stage

Choose paints, masks, and determine the order of operations for metal and plastic models.

Core Development

Sanding, priming, masking, and airbrushing for consistent metallic finish.

Testing

Fit check: ensure parts align and are secure; test durability of bonded elements.

Final Deployment

Display and photography of finished models; archive materials and color codes.
