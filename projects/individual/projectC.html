Inverted Realistic G920 Pedals

Custom inverted pedals meant to utilize the pedals from the G920 and connect through the wheel. The main body will have a welded diamond-plate steel for the main foot-rest, while the main structure will be wood. The inverted design is planned to mimic the pedals in a real vehicle.

Planning Stage

Define mechanical layout, mounting points, and safety features. Prepare welding and materials list.

Core Development

Fabrication of the pedal structure and assembly of mechanical linkages. Prototyping with wood before final metal work.

Testing

Functional testing with G920 and calibration of pedal travel and force feedback.

Final Deployment

Install into sim rig and validate durability under extended use.
