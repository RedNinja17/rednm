G920-Mounted Gauge Cluster

Hybrid analog-digital gauge cluster designed to mount onto a Logitech G920 wheel base. Through an Arduino Uno R4 and ESP32-S3 it will support NeoPixel lighting and servos based on telemetry data to control indicators for speedometer, tachometer, fuel level, coolant temperature, and oil pressure. An analog odometer and small LCD for extra data is also planned. The housing is planned to primarily be 3D-printed with some wood or metal accents and carbon-fiber wrap.

Planning Stage

Designing the housing and selecting microcontrollers and sensors. Initial BOM and wiring diagrams are being drafted.

Core Development

Firmware for ESP32-S3 and integration with Arduino Uno R4 for servo control and NeoPixel driving. 3D model iterations in Fusion 360 are underway.

Testing

Telemetry integration testing with a simulator and live testing on a G920 wheel base.

Final Deployment

Preparing mounting hardware and final assembly for use in a sim-rig.
